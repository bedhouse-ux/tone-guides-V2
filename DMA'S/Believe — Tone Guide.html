Tone Guide

Believe

DMA'S — Cher cover, triple j Like A Version (2016)
Key: E Rig: Digital — Kemper Player + FR-10 Feel: Atmospheric, slow-building indie swell

The Reference Point

DMA'S turned Cher's dance-pop anthem into a spare, emotional indie-rock reinterpretation — starting intimate and atmospheric, then building into a big, wide-open wall of sound by the final chorus. The guitar tone needs to do double duty: airy and clean for the quiet opening, then thickening into a swelling, chorus-and-reverb-soaked wash without ever turning into distortion. This is a dynamics-and-space build, not a gain build.

Tone

Split to single-coil mode, the SA2200 trades its full humbucking warmth for an airy, glassy clarity that suits the song's spare opening — semi-hollow resonance underneath keeps it from feeling thin. Flip the coil-split off (back to full humbucker) for the final chorus if you want extra body and push under the big swell; both settings sit within the same guitar and pickup, so it's a quick switch mid-song rather than a guitar change.

Profile choice: a warm, breakup-leaning British or Vox-style clean profile works well here — similar territory to the Maybe Tomorrow guide, giving the tone some life and roundness rather than a stark, scooped American clean. I don't have visibility into your saved rig library, so pick your best warm clean-to-edge profile and treat these as starting values.

Gain kept low and genuinely clean through the verses — the emotional build in this song comes from arrangement, dynamics, and effects, not amp breakup. Save any extra push for the optional drive stompbox below.
