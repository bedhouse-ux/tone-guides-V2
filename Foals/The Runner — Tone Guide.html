The Runner

Foals — Everything Not Saved Will Be Lost – Part 2 (2019)
Key: D minor (Drop D tuning) Rig: Clapp 15 Feel: Fuzzy, jagged, prowling riff building to an anthemic chorus

The Reference Point

Reviewers described this track as guitars that "prowl" through the verses before something more grandiose sweeps in on the chorus — a gnarlier, dirtier evolution of Foals' usual sound, with real fuzz-pedal character rather than a polished modern high-gain tone. The goal is a cutting, jagged riff voice in Drop D that stays articulate through fuzz, opening up into a bigger, more aggressive chorus.

Mini-humbuckers sit between single-coil clarity and full-humbucker girth — genuinely different from the McCarty or H150, and exactly what this riff needs: bright, articulate, and cutting rather than thick, so it stays legible through the fuzz instead of turning to mush. Volume wide open feeding the Swollen Pickle hard.

Scoop left off — this riff needs midrange presence to stay cutting and jagged rather than the huge, flat power-chord voicing Scoop gives you, which would work against the Firebird's already-bright character here. Crunch engaged for tighter compression on the fuzz, keeping the riff more defined rather than a loose wall of Muff-style saturation. Sustain at 6 gives real fuzz weight without drowning the riff's rhythmic detail.

Class A/B for punch and tightness under the fuzz — this riff wants definition, not the earlier sag of Class A on top of an already-saturated pedal. Mid pushed forward to keep the riff cutting and present through the chorus swell; Bass Defeat off since the 3-band EQ is doing useful shaping work alongside the fuzz's own Filter control. Bright switch on to keep the top end alive once the fuzz starts compressing everything.
