Tone Guide

Spoonman

Soundgarden — Superunknown (1994)
Key: Drop D Rig: Clapp 15 Feel: Percussive, driving, odd-meter grunge riff

The Reference Point

Where Fell on Black Days is a heavy, unhurried 6/4 riff, Spoonman is more percussive and syncopated — the main riff shifts between 7/4 and 4/4 through the song, and the tone needs to feel driving and punchy rather than just thick. Drop D gives the low string extra weight for the riff's chugging character. This wants a bit more openness and top-end bite than Fell on Black Days to keep the odd-meter riff articulate rather than muddy.

Same guitar as Fell on Black Days for consistency across the pair, and the Seymour Duncan '59s give the sustain and thickness this riff needs on the low, dropped string. Bridge pickup throughout — this riff doesn't have Fell on Black Days' contrasting melodic section, so no need to switch. Tone opened up a bit more than the companion guide (8 vs. 7.5) to keep some bite and definition on the syncopated riff, rather than the darker, rounder character that song wants.
