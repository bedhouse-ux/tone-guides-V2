The Last of Us (Main Theme)

Gustavo Santaolalla — HBO Series Main Title Theme (2023)
Key: E minor Rig: Digital — Kemper Player + FR-10 Feel: Sparse, melancholic, fingerpicked melody — reimagined on electric

The Reference Point

Santaolalla's original is performed on a ronroco (a small South American charango-family instrument), fingerpicked and largely dry, minimalist rather than atmospheric. This build reimagines that melody on electric guitar — the goal isn't to copy a ronroco's timbre, which isn't possible here, but to capture the same spare, melancholic character: a clean, resonant tone with just enough space and shimmer to feel cinematic without turning the piece into a wash. Play it fingerstyle rather than with a pick to keep the phrasing close to the source.

Tone at 7 keeps some air on top for the melody to sing.

A moderate analog or tape-style delay gives each plucked note a natural decay and a hint of the resonant quality the ronroco has acoustically. Feedback high enough for a couple of clear repeats, not a wash — this should still read as a solo melodic line, not a pad.

A longer, spacious decay gives the piece the cinematic quality the HBO title sequence wants, without needing any gain or drive to build atmosphere. Mix pushed a bit higher than a typical rhythm-guitar setting, since ambience is doing real emotional work here.
